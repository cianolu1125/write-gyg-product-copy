# Field purpose, tone, and content boundaries

Every field must perform its assigned job. Do not scatter operational instructions or disclaimers through sales copy.

## Product Title — earn the click

- Hard range: 57-60 visible characters.
- Compress the most purchase-influential attraction keywords from the Highlights plus the strongest verified soft-service differentiator.
- Prioritize famous/recognizable sights first, then route scope, then small group, pickup, special access, or a valuable inclusion.
- Keep the activity type clear and avoid number ambiguity.
- Do not include warnings, limitations, meeting instructions, or vague praise.

## Short Description — convert search interest

- Hard range: 190-200 characters in 2-3 flowing sentences.
- Introduce the whole purchase with energy: core route promise, top attraction proof, and service payoff.
- Sound inviting and natural, not like a compressed schedule or disclaimer.
- Do not include weather, traffic, refund, late-arrival, meeting, voucher, or contact instructions.

## Highlights — five reasons to book

- Lines 1-3: rank the most popular and purchase-influential visible attractions or experience outcomes first.
- Lines 4-5: verified service differentiation and additional value.
- Each line must communicate a benefit, not only a place name.
- Keep warnings and execution instructions out.

## Long Description — make guests imagine the journey

- Use chronological nodes while keeping every body vivid, warm, guest-centered, and sales-oriented.
- Introduce what the guest sees, does, feels, photographs, tastes, learns, or gains at each stop.
- Explain why each stop deserves its place in the route and maintain forward momentum.
- Mention pickup, transport, group format, or route design only as verified convenience benefits—not as detailed participation instructions.
- Do not insert weather disclaimers, refund language, late/no-show rules, luggage rules, exact meeting directions, contact instructions, or operational warnings.

## Option Title and Description — make the right choice desirable

- Lead with the option's customer value and concrete difference.
- Keep the Description persuasive and easy to compare with parallel options.
- An option-specific minimum booking size or eligibility condition may appear because it is necessary to choose correctly; phrase it clearly and politely.
- Put detailed participation instructions in Voucher Information and general cautions in Know Before You Go.

## Includes and Not Included — factual purchase boundaries

- Use concise factual items. Do not add decorative adjectives or disclaimers.
- Ensure option-specific differences are not contradicted by product-level lists.

## Voucher Information — how to take the trip

Place post-booking execution information here:

- exact meeting point and recognition cue;
- pickup window and where to wait;
- how early to arrive;
- when/how final driver, vehicle, or pickup details are sent;
- generic email or WhatsApp notification wording;
- voucher, ticket, identification, phone-reachability, or check-in instructions;
- what to do if the message is not received.

Keep it practical, chronological, reassuring, and free of scenic sales copy. Never insert a specific email address or phone number.

## Know Before You Go — prevent surprises politely

Place disclaimers, restrictions, and cautionary information here:

- weather visibility and other non-guaranteed outcomes;
- traffic, route-order, schedule, or seasonal variability;
- mobility, stairs, accessibility, clothing, luggage, age, child-seat, or eligibility rules;
- authoritative cancellation, rescheduling, late-arrival, no-show, and refund conditions;
- driver/guide role or commentary limitations;
- optional costs and material exclusions that require explanation.

Use one issue per bullet. Explain the reason where helpful. Be courteous and tactful, but never weaken the exact condition or invent a policy.

## Final zoning test

- If a sentence helps the guest desire the product, place it in a sales field.
- If it tells the booked guest how to participate, place it in Voucher Information.
- If it warns, limits, disclaims, or prevents surprise, place it in Know Before You Go.
