## Exact Long Description layout

Use this pattern. Do not add a standalone generic introduction before the departure node or a separate sales closing after the return node.

```text
▼ 07:50 - Departure from Tokyo
• 08:00 Meeting Point
• 07:00 - 08:30 Hotel Pickup
Departure body of 200–400 characters, explaining the customer journey and relevant service details.

▼ 10:30 - Hirano Beach at Lake Yamanaka (40 min)
Stop body of 200–400 characters: customer action, concrete sight or experience, why the stop matters, and a truthful transition or caveat.

▼ 16:00 - 18:00 - Return to Tokyo
Return body of 200–400 characters, including the expected arrival information when supplied.
```

Layout requirements:

- Put each `▼` heading on its own line.
- Use the format `▼ time - place/activity (duration)` when time and duration are supplied.
- Under the departure heading, optional meeting/pickup details appear as real bullet paragraphs.
- Put the body immediately after the heading or its optional departure bullets.
- Leave one blank line between itinerary nodes.
- Keep headings, durations, pickup windows, and return times consistent with the source itinerary.
- The validator counts headings, bullet text, and bodies toward the full-description total, but not newline control characters.

Current user instruction overrides the sample's operational placement: exact meeting/pickup times, waiting directions, contact method, and other participation instructions belong in Voucher Information. Keep `details` empty in Long Description unless the text is a brief sales-oriented service label rather than an instruction.

## Other template instructions

### Product Title

Structure: departure/location + colon + core attraction/activity keyword + differentiating feature. The DOCX does not contain a numeric title minimum or maximum.

### Short Description

Write it after Highlights and Long Description. Sentence 1 expands the core USP/title. Then summarize the route and strongest highlights with dense, concrete selling points. The clean sample in the DOCX is 198 characters, but the DOCX does not state a numeric range.

### Highlights

- Lines 1–3: core attractions, popular landmarks, or distinctive niche experiences.
- Lines 4–5: material differentiators such as small-group service, a meal, or hotel pickup, only when confirmed.

### Options

Keep the title concise and make the option difference scannable. In the description, state the inclusion/difference first, an option-specific caution second, and a selling point only if space remains.

### Meeting, pickup, and availability

- Generic standing instruction is permitted: pickup or vehicle details will be sent by email or WhatsApp the day before.
- Do not include a specific email address or WhatsApp number.
- State readiness/early-arrival expectations when supplied.
- Availability must use the actual activity departure time, not the beginning of the pickup window.
